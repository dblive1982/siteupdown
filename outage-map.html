How to read this map

  • Green means almost every site we watch answered from that place. Amber and red mean more of them did not.
  • A whole place turning red can point to a problem with a network or country, not just one website.
  • Some sites deliberately block automated checks from certain countries, so a lower figure is a hint, not proof of an outage.
  • The data comes from probes on the public Globalping network, refreshed through the day. Read how we check for the details.
