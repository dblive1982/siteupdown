Editorial policy.

This page explains how the content on siteupdown.com is produced, how we keep it accurate, and how advertising fits in. We want you to be able to trust what you read here.

What we publish

siteupdown.com has three kinds of content. Live results come from our own automated checks of the sites on our public lists, repeated through the day from our own server and from probes around the world. Guides in the Help centre explain how the internet works and how to fix common problems, such as DNS errors, certificate warnings and slow connections. Site pages (for example the “Is YouTube down?” pages) combine a live status with plain background on the service and recent history.

How we write and check

  • Written for people. Guides are written by the siteupdown.com team in plain English, from practical experience of running website checks and monitoring.
  • Numbers come from our own data. Uptime figures, outage counts and response times are calculated from our own checks. We say how they are measured on How we check and we label them as guides, not official figures.
  • Honest checks. Our checker identifies itself and never pretends to be a browser to get past a site’s protection. If a site blocks automated visitors we say so instead of calling it down.
  • Dated and reviewed. Guides show when they were last updated, and we review them when something they describe changes.

Corrections

If you spot a mistake or a result that looks wrong, tell us through the contact form. We check every report, fix confirmed errors promptly and update the “last updated” date on the page.

Independence

siteupdown.com is not affiliated with, endorsed by or paid by the websites, games or services it checks. A company cannot pay to change its status, its ranking or what we write about it.

How advertising works

The site is free to use and is supported by advertising. Adverts are always clearly separate from our results and guides, and never look like a status result or a button. Advertising cookies are optional and are only used if you allow them in our cookie choices. We do not sell personal data. Advertisers do not see or influence our content.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to hello@siteupdown.com or use the contact page.
