siteupdown.com is an independent website status checker. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of the websites, games or services it checks, and all names and trademarks belong to their owners.

No guarantees

Results are provided as a convenience, as a snapshot at the moment of the check, and without any warranty. A result does not prove that a site is up or down for everyone, and should not be used as a service-level measurement or for critical decisions. We are not liable for loss arising from reliance on a result.

Acceptable use

Please do not use the checker to attack, overload or probe other people’s systems. We limit how often one visitor can run checks, and we only fetch public web addresses on the standard web ports.

Third parties

The internet speed test sends test data between your browser and Cloudflare's public speed-test servers, so Cloudflare sees your address as it would for any website you visit. We do not store your speed results.

The what-is-my-IP page and the game ping test also run in your browser, and talk to Cloudflare and to public cloud servers, so those services see your address as any website would. We do not store your address or results. If you turn on browser notifications for a site, we store your browser's notification address and the site you chose, so we can send you an alert when it goes down or comes back. Turn it off on the same page and we delete it, and we also delete it automatically if your browser stops accepting notifications.

Regional probes run on the public Globalping network. Sign-in with Google uses Google’s own sign-in service, and the site’s fonts (Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono) are loaded from Google Fonts.
