      How reliable is Twitch?

      If Twitch is not loading for you

      • If videos buffer or stop but the site loads, the problem is often your connection speed or the video servers rather than the whole site.
      • Try a lower video quality, or a different device, to see whether the problem follows the account.
      • Restart your router and pause other downloads on your network.
      • Sign out of the app, sign back in, and update it to the latest version.
      • Check whether other streaming sites work. If none do, the problem is your internet connection.

      Questions about Twitch

      Is Twitch down right now?

      Look at the live status box at the top of this page. It shows the result of our latest automatic check of twitch.tv, repeated every few minutes. If it says the site is up but things still fail for you, the problem is probably on your side or limited to one feature or region.

      Why is Twitch not working for me when it works for others?

      A site can fail for some people and not others because of DNS caching, regional servers, your internet provider, a firewall or VPN, or a problem with your device or app. Our guide on why a website can be down for some people and not others explains how to tell which one it is.

      How do I report a problem with Twitch?

      Use the report buttons on this page and choose what is going wrong. Reports are anonymous and unverified, and each visitor can report each problem once every 30 minutes. They are shown as a chart so you can see whether other people are having the same problem.

      How does siteupdown.com check Twitch?

      Our server loads twitch.tv every few minutes, the way a browser would, and records the response code and how long it took. A site is only called down after two server errors in a row. Our page on how the checks work has the details.
