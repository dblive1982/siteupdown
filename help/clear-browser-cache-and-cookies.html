How to clear the cache and cookies for one website

Your browser keeps copies of pages and small files, called the cache, plus cookies that remember who you are. When they go stale they can cause pages that look broken, endless redirects and sign-in problems, even when the website is working.

The quick test first

Open the site in a private or incognito window. If it works there, the cause is something saved in your normal browser: the cache, cookies or an extension.

Clear it for one site

  • Chrome and Edge: click the icon to the left of the address, choose Cookies and site data or Site settings, then Clear data.
  • Firefox: click the padlock, choose Clear cookies and site data.
  • Safari on Mac: Settings, Privacy, Manage Website Data, search for the site and remove it.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, Safari, Advanced, Website Data, search for the site and remove it.
  • Android Chrome: tap the menu, Settings, Site settings, All sites, find the site and clear its data.

A hard refresh

On a computer, press Ctrl+Shift+R (or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac) to reload the page and ignore the saved copy.

What you lose

Clearing a site’s cookies signs you out and removes saved preferences for that site only. Other sites are not touched.

If it still fails

Turn off extensions one by one, then use the website not loading checklist. Running the address through our checker tells you whether the site is down for everyone.

What the cache and cookies actually are

The cache stores copies of files such as images, scripts and stylesheets so pages load faster next time. The problem is that the browser sometimes keeps using an old copy after the site has changed. Cookies are small notes a site stores, such as your login or your preferences. A broken or outdated cookie can leave you stuck in a login loop or looking at the wrong account.

Signs it is worth clearing

  • The page looks broken or half-styled, but works in a private window.
  • You keep being logged out, or a login page reloads without letting you in.
  • You see an old version of a page that others say has changed.
  • Errors such as “too many redirects” appear for just one site.

The quick test first

Open the page in a private or incognito window. If it works there, the cache, cookies or an extension in your normal window is the cause, so clearing just that one site’s data is usually enough. You do not need to wipe everything.

What you will lose

Clearing cookies signs you out of sites and may reset saved preferences. Clearing the cache costs nothing except that the next visit loads a little slower while files download again. Saved passwords are separate and are not removed unless you tick that box.

If it does not help

If the problem remains in a private window and on another device, then it is not your browser. Check the site with the checker to see whether it is down for everyone.
