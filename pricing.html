Questions

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. Open your account, choose Plan, then Manage billing. Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for, and then you go back to Free. We do not charge a cancellation fee.

What happens to my data if I go back to Free?

Nothing is deleted. Your watchlist stays, although you will not be able to add sites beyond the free limit of 10. Text and call alerts stop, your status page is paused until you return to Pro, and reports show the last 30 days again. Email, Telegram, Slack and Discord alerts keep working.

Is my card safe?

Card details are typed only on Stripe’s secure payment page. siteupdown.com never sees or stores your card number.

Why are there limits on texts and calls?

Text messages and calls cost us money for every one we send. The monthly limits (100 texts and 20 calls) stop a site that keeps going up and down from running up a bill, and they are far more than most people use. Alerts are sent on a best-effort basis, so for anything critical keep a second alert channel switched on.

Are the reports a service-level agreement?

No. Reports are built from our own checks and are a good guide to how a site performed, but they are not a guarantee and not a legal record. Read how we check for the method.

Can I try it first?

Everything else on siteupdown.com is free, so you can judge the checks, the alerts and the reports before you pay. If something about a charge looks wrong, email hello@siteupdown.com and we will put it right.
