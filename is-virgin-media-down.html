If Virgin Media is not loading for you

  • Check whether other devices and other websites work. If none do, the problem is your connection rather than one site.
  • Restart your router: switch it off for 30 seconds and back on. Many faults clear this way.
  • Use your phone’s mobile data to look at your provider’s status page or outage checker, which often works even when your home broadband is down.
  • If your mobile has no signal, try switching airplane mode on and off, or restarting the phone.
  • Look at your provider’s official social account, since area outages are often announced there first.

Questions about Virgin Media

Is Virgin Media down right now?

Look at the live status box at the top of this page. It shows the result of our latest automatic check of virginmedia.com, repeated every few minutes. If it says the site is up but things still fail for you, the problem is probably on your side or limited to one feature or region.

Why is Virgin Media not working for me when it works for others?

A site can fail for some people and not others because of DNS caching, regional servers, your internet provider, a firewall or VPN, or a problem with your device or app. Our guide on why a website can be down for some people and not others explains how to tell which one it is.

How do I report a problem with Virgin Media?

Use the report buttons on this page and choose what is going wrong. Reports are anonymous and unverified, and each visitor can report each problem once every 30 minutes. They are shown as a chart so you can see whether other people are having the same problem.

How does siteupdown.com check Virgin Media?

Our server loads virginmedia.com every few minutes, the way a browser would, and records the response code and how long it took. A site is only called down after two server errors in a row. Our page on how the checks work has the details.
