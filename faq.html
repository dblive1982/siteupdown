Frequently asked questions.

Is siteupdown.com free?

Yes. The checker, speed test, tools and status pages are free and need no sign-up. A free account adds a watchlist, uptime history and alerts.

How do I know if a website is down for everyone or just me?

Paste the address into the checker. We load it from our server and from fifteen places around the world. If they can all reach it, the problem is probably on your side.

Why is a website down?

Common causes are a server overloaded by traffic, a failed update, a problem at the hosting company or CDN, an expired domain or certificate, or a DNS fault. Our guide to why big websites go down explains each.

Why does it say No answer or Not sure instead of Down?

A slow or blocked reply is not proof of an outage. We only say Down when there is real evidence, so you are never told something we cannot be sure of.

A site works for me but you say it is down. Why?

The site may be blocking our server, or a problem may affect some regions only. Check the worldwide results, and see why a site can be down for some people and not others.

How often are sites checked?

Popular sites about every five minutes, and watchlist sites about every minute. The live graph updates about every ten seconds while you watch.

Where are the checks made from?

From our own server, and from fifteen probes in different countries on the public Globalping network.

Can I get an alert when my site goes down?

Yes. Add the site to your free watchlist on the Monitor page and choose email, Telegram, Slack or Discord alerts.

Can you check SSL certificates and domain expiry?

Yes. Every check shows the certificate and the domain registration date, and the free tools go deeper.

How can I check if my DNS change has spread?

Use the DNS propagation checker, which asks DNS from fifteen places.

Do you store my searches?

We keep the website name and the result in a public recent-checks list, not who asked. See the privacy page.

Are you affiliated with the sites you check?

No. siteupdown.com is independent, and all names belong to their owners.

Why are there adverts?

Advertising keeps the site free. Advertising cookies are only used if you allow them in our cookie choices.

How do I report a wrong result or suggest a site?

Use the contact page.
