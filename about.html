About.

siteupdown.com answers one question quickly: is this website down for everyone, or is it just you?

What we do

When a page will not load, it is hard to tell whether the site is broken or something on your side is. You paste the address, and we load it from our own server and from fifteen probes in different countries at the same moment. If every probe reaches it, the site is up and the problem is probably your network, DNS, browser or device. If most fail, it is down for everyone.

More than a status check

Every check also shows the SSL certificate, DNS records, redirects and the domain’s registration expiry. The speed test times where a page’s load goes. With a free account you can keep a watchlist, see uptime history and get an email when a site goes down or comes back, and each monitored site has a public status page.

How we check, and how we try to be fair

We would rather say “not sure” than be wrong. Here is what happens when you check a site:

  • Our own server asks the site for its page. If the address gets no reply, we also try the other form (with or without “www”), because some sites only answer on one.
  • Fifteen probes in different countries ask the same thing at the same moment, so you can see whether it is down for everyone or only in some places.
  • We call a site down only on real evidence, such as repeated server errors, or a site that was answering and then stops. Before we record an outage we ask probes in other countries as well, and if any of them can reach it, it is not counted as down.
  • Silence is not proof. Some large sites quietly ignore automated checks. When nothing answers and we have not seen the site working lately, we say “no answer” and “we can’t be sure”, and give you a link to try the site yourself, instead of showing a false red alert.
  • We use honest checks. Our checker says who it is, and we do not disguise it as a browser to get past a site’s protection.

The live graph samples a site about every ten seconds while someone is watching it, and we keep daily records so you can see the last 24 hours, 7 days and 12 months, with a written summary of recent outages. Problem reports from visitors show what real people are seeing, and every result is a snapshot of one moment, which we say so.

Independent

siteupdown.com is not affiliated with the websites, games or services it checks. Regional probes run on the public Globalping network. The site is supported by advertising, so it stays free to use.

What you will find here

  • The website checker, which tests any public address from our server and fifteen places around the world.
  • Live status pages for popular sites, apps and games, with uptime history and problem reports from other visitors.
  • Free tools: DNS lookup, DNS propagation, ping, traceroute, SSL, headers, redirects, WHOIS and more.
  • A help centre of plain-English guides to website errors, DNS, SSL, uptime and speed.
  • A free monitor with email, Telegram, Slack and Discord alerts, for the sites you care about.

Our principles

  • Honest results. A result that is only a guess is labelled as one. Read exactly how in How we check.
  • Original, useful guides. The help articles are written for this site, to help you fix a problem or understand a result.
  • Light on data. We store as little as we can. See the privacy page.
  • Independent. We are not owned by, paid by or connected to the sites we check.
