Extra checks

Go beyond “is it up?”. Check that a word is still on a page (for example your shop’s “Add to basket” button), or that a port such as email or a database is answering. These run every minute, and we alert you after two fails in a row, the same way as your watchlist.

Word checks read the first 600 KB of the page and ignore capital letters. Only check sites and servers you own or look after.

Alert channels

We always email you. You can also get an instant message in Telegram, Slack or Discord when a site on your watchlist goes down or comes back up. Use the Alerts button on each site to switch alerts on or off.

Open Telegram

How do I get a webhook address?

Slack: in Slack, open the Apps menu, add “Incoming Webhooks”, pick a channel and copy the Webhook URL. Discord: open the channel settings, choose Integrations, then Webhooks, create one and copy its URL. Paste the address above and we will send a test message straight away.

Webhook addresses are private, so keep them to yourself. You can remove a channel at any time, and deleting your account removes them all.
